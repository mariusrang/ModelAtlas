**What we changed after this run**

- the verifier no longer flags model ids that come from the question itself (new unit test);
- `LLM_TIMEOUT_SECONDS` is now 300 by default, and the Nginx and UI timeouts for `/agent/ask` were raised;
- results of LLM-written queries are now `HYPOTHESIS` facts (confidence capped at 0.6), not `DERIVED`;
- the query guard also refuses whole-node results, and EXPLAIN plans with a cartesian product or more than
  5 million estimated rows;
- the publisher mistake stays as a known limit: text-to-Cypher can return a valid, read-only query that answers
  a slightly different question, and no guardrail can see that. This is why LLM-written Cypher lowers the
  confidence and why the response always shows the query, so the user can check it.

Two problems of the evaluator itself, from Lucas's analysis of run 1, fixed after this run: `grade()` counted an
expected tool even when the call failed (now the call must succeed), and the mean confidence included `no_answer`
results (now only answered questions). The confidence stays an explainable heuristic, not a calibrated accuracy.
